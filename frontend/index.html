Advancing Materials Innovation through Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

MatAi 2027 provides an international platform for researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology experts, and industry professionals to exchange knowledge and present advances across the rapidly evolving fields of materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

The conference focuses on the convergence of advanced materials research, engineering innovation, computational methodologies, and artificial intelligence, with particular emphasis on emerging approaches to materials discovery, design, modelling, characterization, manufacturing, and sustainable technological development.

Through keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral and poster sessions, technical discussions, and interdisciplinary scientific exchange, MatAi 2027 aims to encourage research collaboration and explore how AI-driven methodologies are shaping the future of materials science and intelligent engineering.

Welcome to MatAi 2027

MatAi 2027 brings together an international community of researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology specialists, and industry professionals to share scientific knowledge and explore emerging developments across materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

As computational technologies and data-driven methodologies continue to advance, artificial intelligence and machine learning are creating new opportunities for materials discovery, design, prediction, characterization, and optimization. MatAi 2027 provides a multidisciplinary platform for discussing these developments and their influence on materials research and engineering applications.

The scientific scope encompasses advanced materials, nanomaterials, polymers and composites, metals and alloys, ceramics, biomaterials, energy materials, smart and multifunctional materials, sustainable engineering, computational materials science, and AI-enabled manufacturing.

The conference welcomes original experimental and theoretical studies, computational investigations, technical contributions, and interdisciplinary research from universities, research institutions, laboratories, and industry. Participants will have opportunities to communicate research findings, exchange perspectives, and establish connections across related scientific disciplines.

Join the international scientific community in Berlin for two days of research presentations, knowledge exchange, and interdisciplinary interaction.

Day 1 Scientific Focus

Day 1 examines how materials are designed, engineered and applied. Sessions move from nanoscale systems to established material families, highlighting relationships between composition, structure, processing and performance.

  • Advanced materials: design, properties and engineering applications
  • Nanomaterials and nanotechnology across research and industry
  • Polymers and composites, including material selection and performance
  • Metals, ceramics and approaches to functional materials
  • Smart materials and responsive material systems
  • Sustainable materials engineering and resource-conscious design
  • Keynote and plenary perspectives, followed by topic sessions and poster discussion
Day 1 brings materials research from fundamental properties to practical engineering.

Day 2 Scientific Focus
