Presentation Categories

How You Can Contribute

🎙️

Oral Presentation

A technical presentation of original research followed by discussion and questions. Intended for contributions with clear research objectives, methodology, findings, and scientific or technical significance.

📌

Poster Presentation

A visual presentation designed to communicate research findings and facilitate focused scientific discussion in a dedicated presentation setting.

💡

Invited Presentation

Specialized contributions presented by researchers invited to participate in selected scientific sessions aligned with the conference program.

🏅

Keynote & Plenary Contributions

Extended presentations addressing significant developments and broader research perspectives within the conference scope, reserved for specially selected contributors.

Preparation Guidelines

Abstract Preparation

  • Clear, concise, and scientifically structured
  • Relevant to the scope of MatAi 2027
  • Based on original research or a substantive technical contribution
  • Written in professional academic English
  • Supported by appropriate scientific information
  • Free from plagiarism and unauthorized third-party material
  • Compliant with applicable research integrity requirements
  • Accompanied by relevant scientific keywords

Recommended Abstract Structure

Title

Concise and representative of the research.

Authors & Affiliations

Full names, institutional affiliations, and corresponding author details.

Background / Context

Brief description of the research problem or scientific context.

Objectives

Clear statement of the purpose or research question.

Methodology / Approach

Summary of the methods, computational approaches, experiments, or techniques used.

Results / Findings

Principal findings or expected outcomes, where applicable.

Conclusion / Significance

Key conclusions and relevance of the research.

Keywords

Relevant scientific keywords identifying the main subject areas.

Submission Requirements

Please ensure your submission includes: abstract title, full author names, institutional affiliations, corresponding author details, abstract text, relevant keywords, preferred presentation category, and selected scientific session or research area. Specific word limits, formatting requirements, and submission fields will be provided through the official submission system.

Presentation Categories & Eligibility

Which Category Fits Your Work

Contributions are accepted across five presentation formats. Oral and poster contributions are open to all qualified submitters through the abstract submission process; keynote, plenary, and invited presentations are extended by the scientific committee.

🎙️

Oral Presentation

Suitable for: researchers, academics, PhD scholars, postdoctoral researchers, and industry professionals presenting original, completed, or advanced-stage research.

📌

Poster Presentation

Suitable for: students, early-career researchers, and professionals presenting focused studies, preliminary findings, or ongoing research.

💡

Invited Presentation

Suitable for: researchers invited to contribute specialized expertise to selected scientific sessions aligned with the conference program.

🌟

Plenary Presentation

Suitable for: established researchers addressing scientific and technological themes of broad relevance across the conference community.

🎤

Keynote Lecture

Suitable for: distinguished experts delivering high-level perspectives on significant developments and emerging research directions.

Final Checklist
