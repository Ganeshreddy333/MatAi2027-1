Before You Register

What's Included & Registration Policies

Please review what your registration includes and the applicable policies.

Registration Includes
  • Access to all keynote, plenary, and scientific sessions across both days.
  • Conference kit, program, and delegate materials.
  • Coffee breaks and lunch on both conference days.
  • Certificate of participation / presentation.
  • Inclusion of accepted abstracts in the conference proceedings (subject to guidelines).

Accommodation, the social & networking event, and accompanying-person access are optional add-ons. Their prices are included in your summary when selected above.

  • Student registration may require valid proof of student status.
  • Abstract acceptance and registration are separate processes.
  • Registration does not guarantee publication.
  • Accepted presenters are expected to participate according to the assigned program.
  • The organizers may make reasonable changes to speakers, sessions, schedules, or venue arrangements.
  • Registration transfers require prior approval.

  • Exhibition and sponsorship benefits are subject to the applicable package and organizer approval.

  • Participants and organizations must comply with the MatAi 2027 Code of Conduct.
  • The organizers reserve the right to refuse or withdraw participation where serious violations of conference policies occur.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Participants who are unable to attend MatAi 2027 may request cancellation according to the following schedule:

Cancellation PeriodRefund
60 days or more before the conference60% refund
50–59 days before the conference50% refund
40–49 days before the conference50% refund
30–39 days before the conference30% refund
Less than 30 days before the conferenceNo refund

Cancellation Procedure. Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing through the official conference contact channel. The effective cancellation date will be the date on which the written request is received by the organizing team.

Refund Processing. Approved refunds will normally be processed through the original payment method where technically possible. Payment gateway, bank, transaction, currency conversion, and applicable administrative charges may be deducted from the refundable amount.

Non-Refundable Expenses. The following are generally not covered by the registration refund: visa expenses, travel expenses, accommodation, travel insurance, bank charges, currency conversion charges, third-party service charges, and other personal expenses.

Exhibition & Sponsorship Cancellation. Exhibition and sponsorship cancellations are subject to the same published cancellation schedule unless a separate written agreement specifies different terms. Benefits already delivered or services already provided may not be refundable.

Conference Postponement or Cancellation. If MatAi 2027 is postponed, relocated, or cancelled due to circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the organizers, registered participants, exhibitors, and sponsors will be informed of the available arrangements.

Important Registration Notice

Early Bird, Mid-Term, and Late registration deadlines and fees are set by the organizers and may be updated in Admin. The applicable fee is determined by the registration period active when the registration is completed.

Information provided during registration may be used for conference administration, communication, scheduling, certificates, and related organizational purposes in accordance with the Privacy Policy.
