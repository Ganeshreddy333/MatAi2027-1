Abstract Submission

Abstract Submission Guidelines

MatAi 2027 welcomes original scientific and technical contributions covering materials science, engineering, artificial intelligence, computational research, materials informatics, advanced manufacturing, and related interdisciplinary fields. Abstracts may be submitted under the Keynote, Plenary, Invited, Oral, and Poster presentation categories.

Submissions should present original research, significant technical findings, experimental or theoretical studies, computational investigations, methodological developments, or relevant interdisciplinary work.

Abstract Preparation

Title

A concise and informative title reflecting the research.

Authors & Affiliations

Full names of all authors, institutional affiliations, city, and country.

Background / Context

Briefly describe the scientific context and research problem.

Objectives

Clearly state the principal objectives or research questions.

Methodology

Summarize the experimental, theoretical, computational, analytical, or data-driven methods used.

Results / Findings

Present the principal results, observations, or scientific findings.

Conclusion

Summarize the main conclusions and scientific significance.

Keywords

Provide approximately 3–6 relevant keywords.

Submission Requirements

  • Abstracts should be written in clear scientific English.
  • The title should accurately represent the submitted research.
  • Author names and affiliations should be checked carefully.
  • Submitted work should be original.
  • Plagiarized, fabricated, or misleading content will not be accepted.
  • References may be included where appropriate.
  • Tables, figures, equations, or special formatting should follow the submission instructions.
  • The presenting author should be clearly identified.
  • Abstracts should be submitted through the official submission process.

Abstract Review

Submitted abstracts may be evaluated based on scientific quality, originality, relevance to the conference scope, technical significance, clarity, methodological soundness, and potential contribution to the field. The scientific committee may accept an abstract, request clarification or revision, recommend an alternative presentation category, or decline a submission that does not meet the conference requirements.

Please Note

Abstract submission does not guarantee acceptance or a specific presentation category.

Speaker Guidelines

Speaker Guidelines

MatAi 2027 aims to maintain a structured and professional environment for scientific presentations across Keynote Lectures, Plenary Lectures, Invited Talks, Oral Presentations, and Poster Presentations.

Speaker Preparation

  • Prepare content relevant to the accepted topic.
  • Present scientifically accurate information.
  • Clearly identify authors and institutional affiliations.
  • Acknowledge collaborators and funding sources where applicable.
  • Use appropriate scientific references and avoid unsupported claims.
  • Submit presentation materials within the communicated timeframe.
  • Follow instructions from the session chair and technical team.
  • Arrive at the designated presentation area before the session.

Presentation format, technical requirements, session assignment, and allocated presentation time will be communicated to accepted speakers by the conference organizers.

Speaker Checklist

Before the conference, speakers should confirm their presentation title, author information, affiliation, presentation category, session assignment, presentation file, audio/video requirements, technical compatibility, and registration status.

Presentation Guidelines

Oral Presentation Guidelines

Presenters are encouraged to organize their presentation in a clear scientific sequence:

  • Title & Author Information
  • Research Background
  • Research Problem
  • Objectives
  • Methodology
  • Results
  • Discussion
  • Conclusions
  • Future Research Directions
  • Acknowledgements

Presentations should remain focused on the accepted research topic and comply with the presentation time communicated by the organizers.

Technical Preparation

Presenters should ensure that presentation files open correctly, fonts and graphics display correctly, figures and tables are readable, videos and animations function properly, audio is tested where applicable, and the presentation is available in the required file format. Presenters should coordinate with the technical team before their session when technical assistance is required.

Presentation Guidelines

Poster Presentation Guidelines

Poster presentations provide an opportunity to communicate research findings through a concise visual format.

Recommended Poster Structure

  • Title, Authors & Affiliations
  • Background & Objectives
  • Methodology
  • Results & Discussion
  • Conclusions
  • References & Acknowledgements

Poster Display

  • Display the poster in the assigned location.
  • Follow the poster dimensions communicated by the organizers.
  • Be available during the designated poster session.
  • Engage professionally with delegates and researchers.
  • Remove the poster according to organizer instructions.

Posters should use clear section headings, readable text, appropriate figures and diagrams, relevant charts and tables, and concise scientific explanations.

Detailed poster dimensions and technical specifications will be communicated to accepted presenters.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

MatAi 2027 respects the privacy of participants, speakers, authors, sponsors, exhibitors, and other stakeholders.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the form you use, information may include your name, email address, phone number, institution or organization, country, registration category and selected items, accommodation or billing details, abstract and uploaded document, contact message, and brochure request details. Information is used to administer registrations and payment status, review abstracts, respond to enquiries, provide materials you request, coordinate conference activities, and protect the service. Brochure update consent is optional; it is not a subscription-management or marketing-preference system.

Submission data is stored in the conference application database or private application files and is accessible to authorized administrators and hosting operators. If enabled, Stripe, PayPal, or Razorpay processes payment information under its own terms; the configured mail and hosting providers also process information needed to deliver the service. Google Fonts and Bootstrap CSS are loaded from third-party CDNs, and map links are opened only when selected. Those providers may receive request metadata such as your IP address. Processing locations and provider configuration depend on the deployed service.

Retention and Your Requests

Retention periods and deletion procedures are being finalized with the conference operator and applicable legal advisers. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the organizing team using the official contact details on this site and include enough information for the team to locate your record. Requests may be subject to identity verification and legal, accounting, conference, or backup-retention requirements. Do not email payment card details or sensitive documents.

This notice requires review and approval by the conference operator and qualified legal counsel before launch. It is not a representation of legal compliance. Questions about privacy or a suspected data incident should be sent to the organizing team through the official contact channel.
