Sponsors & Exhibition

Partner with MatAi 2027

International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

October 13–14, 2027 | Berlin, Germany

MatAi 2027 brings together researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology specialists, industry professionals, and R&D organizations working across Materials Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Materials Informatics, Computational Science, Advanced Manufacturing, and Sustainable Materials.

The Sponsors & Exhibition Programme provides organizations with opportunities to present technologies, research capabilities, products, services, and solutions to an international scientific and technical audience.

Whether your organization is an established technology company, research institution, scientific equipment provider, software developer, advanced materials manufacturer, engineering organization, or emerging technology company, MatAi 2027 offers flexible ways to participate.

WHY PARTNER WITH MatAi 2027?

Partnering with MatAi 2027 provides opportunities to:

  • Present your organization to an international scientific audience
  • Increase visibility among researchers and technical professionals
  • Showcase products, technologies, equipment, and services
  • Connect with universities and research institutions
  • Engage with academic and industrial R&D communities
  • Develop professional and research collaborations
  • Participate in technical and industry discussions
  • Demonstrate emerging technologies and solutions
  • Strengthen your presence within the materials and AI ecosystem
  • Build relationships with potential research and industry partners

EXHIBITION GUIDELINES

Exhibition Hours

Exhibition activities will be scheduled around the scientific programme, coffee breaks, lunch, networking periods, and designated exhibition hours.

Exhibitor Responsibilities

Exhibitors are responsible for:

  • Providing approved promotional materials
  • Providing product samples where applicable
  • Following venue regulations
  • Meeting installation and dismantling deadlines
  • Ensuring their display complies with safety requirements
  • Arranging shipment and delivery of materials where required

Detailed technical and logistical instructions will be provided to confirmed exhibitors.

SPONSORSHIP TERMS

Pricing

All sponsorship and exhibition fees are stated in USD ($).

Applicable taxes, VAT, venue charges, payment processing fees, or other statutory charges, if applicable, will be communicated in the sponsorship agreement or invoice.

Availability

Sponsorship categories and exhibition spaces are subject to availability.

Premium positions may be allocated on the basis of confirmed booking and payment.

Logo & Artwork

Sponsors must provide logos and artwork in the required formats and within the deadlines specified by the Conference Secretariat.

Advertising Materials

All advertisements, promotional materials, banners, digital assets, and programme artwork are subject to organizer approval and must be submitted according to the specified artwork guidelines and deadlines.

Speaking Opportunities

Industry presentations are subject to programme approval and should contain relevant technical, scientific, engineering, or industry content.

Cancellation

Sponsorship and exhibition cancellation terms will be governed by the applicable sponsorship agreement.
