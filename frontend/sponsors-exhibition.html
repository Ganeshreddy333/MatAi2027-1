Advancing Materials Innovation through Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

Become a Sponsor or Exhibitor

The International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence (MatAi 2027) invites companies, technology providers, research organizations, academic institutions, manufacturers, and industry leaders to participate as sponsors and exhibitors.

MatAi 2027 will bring together researchers, scientists, engineers, academicians, industry professionals, innovators, and emerging researchers to exchange knowledge and explore advances in materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

The conference offers an opportunity for organizations to showcase their products, technologies, services, research capabilities, and innovations to an international audience interested in advanced materials, intelligent engineering, computational science, and AI-driven materials discovery.

By supporting MatAi 2027, your organization can contribute to scientific exchange, strengthen professional relationships, and connect with a community working to advance materials innovation and engineering technologies.

Join us as a sponsor or exhibitor and become part of the materials innovation community.

Why Sponsor MatAi 2027?

Sponsoring MatAi 2027 provides organizations with an opportunity to strengthen their visibility, engage with a specialized scientific audience, and demonstrate their commitment to research, technology, and innovation.

Key Sponsorship Benefits

  • Brand Visibility: Increase your organization's visibility among international researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, and industry professionals.
  • Global Networking: Build connections with potential collaborators, institutional representatives, technology providers, and research groups.
  • Technology Showcase: Present your latest products, materials, instruments, software, equipment, and engineering solutions.
  • Research and Industry Engagement: Connect with experts working in materials science, AI-enabled research, manufacturing, and advanced engineering.
  • Business Development: Explore opportunities for collaboration, technology adoption, professional partnerships, and future business relationships.
  • Thought Leadership: Demonstrate your organization's expertise and commitment to scientific progress and technological development.
  • Recruitment and Talent Engagement: Interact with emerging researchers, postgraduate scholars, and professionals with relevant technical expertise.

Exhibition Opportunities

MatAi 2027 welcomes exhibitors interested in presenting innovative products, services, technologies, and solutions relevant to materials science, engineering, artificial intelligence, and related industries.

The exhibition will provide an opportunity to introduce your organization's capabilities, engage directly with conference participants, and explore potential collaborations.

Who Can Exhibit?

Exhibition opportunities are suitable for:

  • Materials science and engineering companies
  • Advanced materials and nanotechnology organizations
  • Scientific instruments and laboratory equipment manufacturers
  • Artificial intelligence, machine learning, and materials informatics companies
  • Simulation, modelling, and computational engineering providers
  • Semiconductor, electronics, and photonics companies
  • Additive manufacturing and 3D-printing technology providers
  • Energy storage, renewable energy, and sustainable technology companies
  • Testing, characterization, and analytical service providers
  • Research institutions, universities, laboratories, and technology-transfer organizations
  • Scientific publishers and research-support service providers

What Can You Showcase?

Exhibitors may present relevant products, services, and innovations, including:

  • Advanced materials, composites, polymers, ceramics, and alloys
  • Nanomaterials and functional materials
  • AI-driven materials discovery and design platforms
  • Machine learning and computational materials science solutions
  • Materials characterization and analytical technologies
  • Manufacturing, processing, and additive manufacturing systems
  • Energy materials and sustainable engineering solutions
  • Research software, simulation tools, and digital engineering platforms
  • Laboratory equipment, testing systems, and measurement technologies
  • Scientific publications, research services, and institutional capabilities

All exhibits should be relevant to the conference themes and subject to the organizer's approval.
