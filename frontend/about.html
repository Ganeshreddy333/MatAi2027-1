About the Conference

MatAi 2027 — International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence · October 13–14, 2027 · Berlin, Germany.

About the Conference

MatAi 2027 — International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence — will be held on October 13–14, 2027, in Berlin, Germany, bringing together an international community of researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, and industry professionals working across materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

The conference is dedicated to examining emerging developments at the intersection of advanced materials research, engineering innovation, computational science, and artificial intelligence. It provides a multidisciplinary platform for presenting significant research findings, exchanging scientific knowledge, and discussing evolving methodologies and technologies that are shaping the future of materials development.

Particular emphasis is placed on the growing application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, materials informatics, computational modelling, and data-driven approaches to materials discovery, design, characterization, processing, and manufacturing. The conference also encompasses developments in advanced materials, nanomaterials, polymers and composites, metals and alloys, ceramics, biomaterials, energy materials, smart materials, and sustainable materials engineering.

MatAi 2027 welcomes original research, technical contributions, experimental and theoretical studies, computational investigations, and interdisciplinary perspectives from academia, research institutions, and industry. Through keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral and poster sessions, and scientific discussions, the conference aims to facilitate meaningful exchange across disciplines and encourage international research collaboration.

By bringing together expertise from materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence, MatAi 2027 seeks to provide a focused international forum for advancing knowledge, exploring emerging research directions, and addressing the scientific and technological challenges associated with next-generation materials and intelligent engineering.
