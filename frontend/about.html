About the Conference

International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence (MatAi 2027)

October 13–14, 2027 | Berlin, Germany

Conference Theme: Advancing Materials Innovation through Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

About the Conference

MatAi 2027 — International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence — will be held on October 13–14, 2027, in Berlin, Germany, bringing together an international community of researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, and industry professionals working across materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

The conference is dedicated to examining emerging developments at the intersection of advanced materials research, engineering innovation, computational science, and artificial intelligence. It provides a multidisciplinary platform for presenting significant research findings, exchanging scientific knowledge, and discussing evolving methodologies and technologies that are shaping the future of materials development.

Particular emphasis is placed on the growing application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, materials informatics, computational modelling, and data-driven approaches to materials discovery, design, characterization, processing, and manufacturing. The conference also encompasses developments in advanced materials, nanomaterials, polymers and composites, metals and alloys, ceramics, biomaterials, energy materials, smart materials, and sustainable materials engineering.

MatAi 2027 welcomes original research, technical contributions, experimental and theoretical studies, computational investigations, and interdisciplinary perspectives from academia, research institutions, and industry. Through keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral and poster sessions, and scientific discussions, the conference aims to facilitate meaningful exchange across disciplines and encourage international research collaboration.

By bringing together expertise from materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence, MatAi 2027 seeks to provide a focused international forum for advancing knowledge, exploring emerging research directions, and addressing the scientific and technological challenges associated with next-generation materials and intelligent engineering.

Conference Theme

Advancing Materials Innovation through Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

The theme of MatAi 2027 highlights the growing importance of combining scientific knowledge, engineering innovation, and artificial intelligence to address complex challenges in modern materials research.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning are increasingly being explored for materials discovery, property prediction, computational modelling, characterization, and manufacturing optimization. When combined with experimental research and engineering expertise, these technologies offer opportunities to improve research efficiency, accelerate scientific investigation, and support the development of advanced functional materials.

MatAi 2027 will provide a forum to explore these developments, discuss emerging research methodologies, and examine future opportunities at the intersection of materials science, engineering, and intelligent technologies.

Scientific Scope

About the Scientific Scope

The conference will cover a broad range of scientific and technological areas, including advanced materials, nanomaterials and nanotechnology, polymers, composite materials, metals and alloys, advanced ceramics, smart and multifunctional materials, sustainable materials, energy storage materials, renewable energy materials, biomaterials, semiconductor materials, electronic materials, and emerging functional materials.

The program will also address artificial intelligence in materials science, machine learning, materials informatics, AI-driven materials discovery and design, computational materials science, multiscale modelling, predictive materials engineering, and AI-assisted materials characterization.

Additional areas of interest include additive manufacturing, intelligent manufacturing, digital twins, advanced materials processing, surface engineering, materials for environmental and climate technologies, materials for aerospace and automotive applications, materials reliability, autonomous laboratories, and AI-enabled experimental research.

Through this diverse scientific scope, MatAi 2027 aims to encourage interdisciplinary discussions connecting fundamental research, computational science, engineering applications, and emerging industrial technologies.
