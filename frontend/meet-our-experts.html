Scientific Committee Members

Supporting Scientific Excellence and Academic Integrity

The International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence (MatAi 2027) recognizes the importance of scientific expertise, academic excellence, and interdisciplinary collaboration in advancing research and innovation. The Scientific Committee plays an important role in supporting the academic direction and scientific development of the conference.

MatAi 2027 aims to bring together researchers, scientists, academicians, engineers, and industry professionals working across materials science, engineering, computational research, and artificial intelligence. The Scientific Committee provides an academic foundation for the conference by contributing relevant expertise and perspectives across these interconnected disciplines.

About the Scientific Committee

The Scientific Committee comprises experts whose academic backgrounds, research experience, and professional interests are relevant to the conference's scientific scope. Its members represent diverse research areas, including advanced materials, nanotechnology, polymers, composites, functional materials, sustainable engineering, computational materials science, machine learning, and AI-enabled materials research.

Through their scientific knowledge and professional experience, committee members help support the development of a comprehensive scientific program that reflects emerging research directions and current challenges in the field. Their participation encourages meaningful academic exchange and helps connect different areas of scientific and engineering research.

Advancing Research Through Collaboration

MatAi 2027 aims to encourage scientific communication, academic cooperation, and interdisciplinary research. The involvement of experienced researchers and academic professionals supports these objectives by bringing diverse scientific perspectives into the conference environment.

We acknowledge the value of scientific expertise and academic participation in encouraging research excellence and advancing materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

We look forward to fostering productive scientific discussions and meaningful collaboration through MatAi 2027, scheduled for October 13–14, 2027, in Berlin, Germany.
