Conference Speaker Overview

MatAi 2027 brings together an international community of researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, and industry professionals to present and discuss developments across Materials Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and related interdisciplinary fields.

The conference speaker program is structured to provide a balanced scientific perspective through Keynote Lectures, Plenary Presentations, Invited Talks, Oral Presentations, and Poster Presentations. Contributions will address established research areas as well as emerging developments in advanced materials, materials engineering, computational materials science, artificial intelligence, machine learning, materials informatics, and AI-enabled materials research.

The program is intended to facilitate high-level scientific exchange, interdisciplinary discussion, and knowledge sharing among participants from universities, research institutions, laboratories, and industry.
