Scientific Committee

Established researchers, academics, scientists, and subject-matter experts representing Materials Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and related disciplines.

The MatAi 2027 Scientific Committee brings together established researchers, academics, scientists, and subject-matter experts representing the broad fields of Materials Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and related disciplines.

The committee provides scientific direction for the conference and supports the development of a rigorous, relevant, and interdisciplinary scientific program.

Responsibilities

Committee Responsibilities

  • Providing scientific and technical guidance
  • Supporting the development of conference sessions and research tracks
  • Advising on emerging areas within Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence
  • Supporting the review and evaluation of submitted abstracts
  • Contributing to the scientific quality and relevance of the program
  • Encouraging international and interdisciplinary participation
  • Supporting academic and industry engagement
