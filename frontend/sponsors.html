Partner with MatAi 2027

Showcase your technology, products, and research to an international community of researchers, engineers, and industry professionals. October 13–14, 2027 · Berlin, Germany. All fees are stated in EUR (€).

MatAi 2027 brings together researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology specialists, industry professionals, and R&D organizations working across Materials Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Materials Informatics, Computational Science, Advanced Manufacturing, and Sustainable Materials.

The Sponsors & Exhibition Programme provides organizations with opportunities to present technologies, research capabilities, products, services, and solutions to an international scientific and technical audience.

Whether your organization is an established technology company, research institution, scientific equipment provider, software developer, advanced materials manufacturer, engineering organization, or emerging technology company, MatAi 2027 offers flexible ways to participate.

Exhibition Guidelines

Planning Your Exhibition

Exhibition Hours

Exhibition spaces will operate during official conference hours across both days of the programme.

  • Setup during the designated pre-conference period
  • Exhibition open throughout conference hours (09:00 AM – 06:30 PM)
  • Access during breaks, lunch, and networking periods
  • Dismantling after the close of the final session

Exhibitor Responsibilities

  • Staffing the booth during exhibition hours
  • Providing branding, signage, and display materials
  • Observing venue regulations and safety instructions
  • Coordinating shipping, delivery, and setup logistics
  • Respecting neighbouring booths and shared spaces
  • Following on-site instructions from the Secretariat

Detailed exhibitor guidelines, setup schedules, and technical specifications will be provided following confirmation of participation.
