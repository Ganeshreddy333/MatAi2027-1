About the Conference

MatAi 2027 brings together researchers, engineers, and industry leaders advancing the convergence of materials science, engineering, and artificial intelligence. Over two days in Berlin, delegates share breakthroughs across 30 scientific sessions, keynote lectures, poster presentations, and industry showcases.

Who Should Attend

  • Materials scientists & engineers
  • AI / ML and computational-materials researchers
  • Industry R&D professionals and innovators
  • Postgraduate students & early-career researchers
