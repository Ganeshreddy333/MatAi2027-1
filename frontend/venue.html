Berlin, Germany

October 13–14, 2027 · A professional environment for keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral communications, poster sessions, technical discussions, networking, and industry–academic interaction.

MatAi 2027 will be held in Berlin, Germany, bringing together researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology professionals, and industry representatives for two days of scientific presentations, technical exchange, and interdisciplinary discussion.

The conference venue will provide a professional environment for keynote and plenary lectures, invited presentations, oral communications, poster sessions, technical discussions, networking activities, and industry–academic interaction.

Designed for Scientific Exchange

🏛️

Main Conference Auditorium

A dedicated auditorium for the Opening Ceremony, keynote and plenary lectures, special lectures, panel discussions, and the Closing Ceremony.

🖥️

Technical Presentation Rooms

Dedicated rooms for parallel technical sessions, invited presentations, and oral communications — with projection, audio, microphones, Wi-Fi, and technical assistance.

📌

Poster Presentation Area

Dedicated space for researchers and early-career scientists to present their work and engage directly with participants, supporting informal scientific discussion.

Registration & Information Desk

Participant registration, badge collection, programme information, venue directions, speaker and session assistance, and certificate information.

🎤

Speaker Preparation Area

A space for invited and oral presenters to review presentations, test files, coordinate with technical staff, and confirm session arrangements.

🤝

Networking & Discussion Areas

Suitable areas for research discussions, academic networking, industry interaction, and speaker–participant connections.

🏢

Exhibition & Sponsor Area

Space for organizations, research institutions, technology companies, publishers, and service providers to showcase technologies and solutions.

Catering & Refreshments

Accessibility

Accessible entrances, step-free access where available, accessible restrooms, elevator access, and accessible seating arrangements.
