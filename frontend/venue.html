Welcome to MatAi 2027

The International Conference on Materials Science, Engineering & Artificial Intelligence (MatAi 2027) will take place on October 13–14, 2027, in Berlin, Germany. The conference will bring together researchers, scientists, engineers, academics, technology professionals, and industry representatives for two days of scientific presentations, knowledge exchange, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Berlin offers an international setting for scientific engagement, innovation, and professional networking. MatAi 2027 aims to provide a welcoming environment where participants can present their research, discuss emerging technologies, explore new ideas, and develop collaborations across materials science, engineering, artificial intelligence, and computational science.
