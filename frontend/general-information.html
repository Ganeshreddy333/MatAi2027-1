ABSTRACT SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Call for Abstracts

MatAi 2027 welcomes original scientific and technical contributions covering materials science, engineering, artificial intelligence, computational research, materials informatics, advanced manufacturing, and related interdisciplinary fields.

Abstracts may be submitted for consideration under the following presentation categories:

  • Keynote Presentation
  • Plenary Presentation
  • Invited Presentation
  • Oral Presentation
  • Poster Presentation

Submissions should present original research, significant technical findings, experimental or theoretical studies, computational investigations, methodological developments, or relevant interdisciplinary work.

Abstract Preparation

Authors should prepare abstracts using the following structure:

Title
A concise and informative title reflecting the research.

Authors & Affiliations
Full names of all authors, institutional affiliations, city, and country.

Background / Research Context
Briefly describe the scientific context and research problem.

Objectives
Clearly state the principal objectives or research questions.

Methodology
Summarize the experimental, theoretical, computational, analytical, or data-driven methods used.

Results / Findings
Present the principal results, observations, or scientific findings.

Conclusion
Summarize the main conclusions and scientific significance.

Keywords
Provide approximately 3–6 relevant keywords.

Submission Requirements

  • Abstracts should be written in clear scientific English.
  • The title should accurately represent the submitted research.
  • Author names and affiliations should be checked carefully.
  • Submitted work should be original.
  • Plagiarized, fabricated, or misleading content will not be accepted.
  • References may be included where appropriate.
  • Tables, figures, equations, or special formatting should follow the submission instructions.
  • The presenting author should be clearly identified.
  • Abstracts should be submitted through the official submission process.

Abstract Review

Submitted abstracts may be evaluated based on:

  • Scientific quality
  • Originality
  • Relevance to the conference scope
  • Technical significance
  • Clarity
  • Methodological soundness
  • Potential contribution to the field

The scientific committee may accept an abstract, request clarification or revision, recommend an alternative presentation category, or decline a submission that does not meet the conference requirements.

Abstract submission does not guarantee acceptance or a specific presentation category.

PRESENTATION GUIDELINES

Oral Presentation Guidelines

Presenters are encouraged to organize their presentation in a clear scientific sequence:

  1. Title & Author Information
  2. Research Background
  3. Research Problem
  4. Objectives
  5. Methodology
  6. Results
  7. Discussion
  8. Conclusions
  9. Future Research Directions
  10. Acknowledgements

Presentations should remain focused on the accepted research topic and comply with the presentation time communicated by the organizers.

Technical Preparation

Presenters should ensure that:

  • Presentation files open correctly.
  • Fonts and graphics display correctly.
  • Figures and tables are readable.
  • Videos and animations function properly.
  • Audio is tested where applicable.
  • The presentation is available in the required file format.

Presenters should coordinate with the technical team before their session when technical assistance is required.

Poster Presentation Guidelines

Poster presentations provide an opportunity to communicate research findings through a concise visual format.

Recommended Poster Structure

  • Title
  • Authors
  • Affiliations
  • Background
  • Objectives
  • Methodology
  • Results
  • Discussion
  • Conclusions
  • References
  • Acknowledgements

Posters should use:

  • Clear section headings
  • Readable text
  • Appropriate figures and diagrams
  • Relevant charts and tables
  • Concise scientific explanations

Poster Display

Presenters should:

  • Display the poster in the assigned location.
  • Follow the poster dimensions communicated by the organizers.
  • Be available during the designated poster session.
  • Engage professionally with delegates and researchers.
  • Remove the poster according to organizer instructions.

Detailed poster dimensions and technical specifications will be communicated to accepted presenters.
